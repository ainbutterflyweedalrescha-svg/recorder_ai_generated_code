# Support Ticket #RF-418: Register Screen Stutter on Counter Terminals
**Reported by:** Clara Novak, Store Support Lead | **Locations:** Harbor Street Market, Elmwood Corner Grocer, Beacon Hill Provisions
Cashiers across all three shops report that the Register screen stutters on the low-end counter terminals during checkout.
**Steps to reproduce:**
1. Open the Register screen on a counter terminal and add three or four products to the cart.
2. Type a product name or SKU (such as `oat` or `BAK-104`) into the search box at normal speed.
3. Tap the `+` or `-` quantity buttons several times in a row on any item in the cart panel.
**Expected behaviour:** Search box typing and cart quantity adjustments should update smoothly and immediately on every input without delay or stutter.
